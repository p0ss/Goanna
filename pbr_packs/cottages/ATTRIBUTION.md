# Attribution for these maps

Built by tools/pbr_author/ from the textures of the mod Cottages by
Sokomine (ContentDB release 22105, <https://github.com/Sokomine/cottages>).
The normal, occlusion, height and specular maps are derived from height
and smoothness fields authored on the mod's art; block sets also carry that
art upscaled without repainting. They are derivative works of that art, not
new art, and each is offered under the licence of its source texture, per
the mod's README.md, with the authors it names: VanessaE, badger436,
Sokomine, celeron55 and Zeg9 (CC BY-SA 3.0), MasterGollum and PilzAdam
(WTFPL), and the slate derived from Universal schema.jpg by Stefanie
Lindener (CC BY-SA 2.0 DE). cottages_stone and cottages_wool are recorded
under CC BY-SA 3.0, as their pixels are Minetest Game's under that licence,
although the README names them WTFPL. The per texture record is
pbr_packs/manifests/cottages-v1.sources.json.

The rope (credited only "CC"), the unnamed dirt road textures and the loam
(art not a supported size) are not included.
