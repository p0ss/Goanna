# Attribution for these maps

Built by tools/pbr_author/ from the textures of the mod Goblins by
Francisco "FreeLikeGNU" Athens (ContentDB release 32959,
<https://gitlab.com/freelikegnu/goblins>). The normal, occlusion, height
and specular maps are derived from height and smoothness fields authored
on the mod's art; block sets also carry that art upscaled without
repainting. They are derivative works of the mod's art, not new art, and
are offered under the same licence: Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported,
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/>.
