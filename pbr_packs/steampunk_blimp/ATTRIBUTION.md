<https://github.com/APercy/steampunk_blimp>). The normal, occlusion,

art, and are offered under the same licence as the mod's media: Creative
Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported,
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/>. The mod's README.md
gives "Blimp model and textures by APercy. CC BY-SA 3.0". The models are
read only for their UV layout and are not copied. Most of the blimp's hull
is drawn with the host game's own textures, which are not part of this
pack.
