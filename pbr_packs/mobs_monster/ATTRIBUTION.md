# Attribution for these maps

Built by tools/pbr_author/ from the textures of the mod Mobs Monster by TenPlus1
and contributors (ContentDB release 38244,
<https://codeberg.org/tenplus1/mobs_monster>). The normal, occlusion, height and
specular maps are derived from height and smoothness fields authored on each
texture's art; creature skins ship maps only, block sets also carry the art
upscaled without repainting. They are derivative works of that art, not new art,
and each is offered under the licence of its source texture, with the authors
named below, per the mod's license.txt. The per texture record is
pbr_packs/manifests/mobs-monster-v1.sources.json. Textures license.txt does not
name, or names under licences outside Goanna's media policy, are not included.
The models are read only for their UV layout and are not copied.
