# Attribution for these maps

Built by tools/pbr_author/ from the textures of the mod Bees by TenPlus1
(ContentDB release 38367, <https://codeberg.org/tenplus1/bees>). The
normal, occlusion, height and specular maps are derived from height and
smoothness fields authored on the mod's art, and the block sets carry that
art upscaled without repainting. They are derivative works of that art,
not new art, and are offered under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
3.0 Unported, <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/>.

The mod's README says "Textures by Bas80 and Neuromancer are CC BY-SA",
naming no version and no files; the package declares CC BY-SA 3.0 on
ContentDB, which supplies the version. Every texture is credited to both
Bas80 and Neuromancer.
