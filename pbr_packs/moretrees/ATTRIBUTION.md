# Attribution for these maps

Built by tools/pbr_author/ from the textures of the mod More Trees by mt-mods
(ContentDB release 38501, <https://github.com/mt-mods/moretrees>). The normal,
occlusion, height and specular maps are derived from height and smoothness
fields authored on the mod's art, and the block sets carry that art upscaled
without repainting. They are derivative works of that art, not new art, and are
offered under the same licence as the mod's media, which its LICENSE gives as
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0,
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>, with the authors it names
below. Rogier's and Tim Huppertz's textures were first published under CC BY-SA
3.0, which permits adaptations under 4.0. The per texture record is
pbr_packs/manifests/moretrees-v1.sources.json.
