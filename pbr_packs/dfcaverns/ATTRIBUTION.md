# Attribution for these maps

Built by tools/pbr_author/ from the textures of the mod DF Caverns (Dwarf
Fortress style caverns) by FaceDeer and contributors (ContentDB release 22198,
<https://github.com/FaceDeer/dfcaverns>). The normal, occlusion, height and
specular maps are derived from height and smoothness fields authored on the
mod's art, and the block sets carry that art upscaled without repainting. They
are derivative works of that art, not new art, and each is offered under the
licence of its source texture, with the authors named below. The per texture
record, with each notice, is pbr_packs/manifests/dfcaverns-v1.sources.json.
