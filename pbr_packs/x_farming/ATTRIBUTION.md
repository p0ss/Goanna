# Attribution for these maps

Built by tools/pbr_author/ from the textures of the mod X Farming by SaKeL
(ContentDB release 38796, <https://bitbucket.org/minetest_gamers/x_farming>).
The normal, occlusion, height and specular maps are derived from height and
smoothness fields authored on the mod's art; block sets also carry that art
upscaled without repainting, and textures on meshes ship maps only. They are
derivative works of that art, not new art, and each is offered under the licence
of its source texture, per the mod's LICENSE.txt credits, with the authors named
below. The per texture record is pbr_packs/manifests/x-farming-v1.sources.json.
The models are read only for their UV layout and are not copied.
