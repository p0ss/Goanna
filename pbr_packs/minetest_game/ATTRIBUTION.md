Generated by tools/pbr_bake.py from the textures of the game at
`/home/poss/.var/app/org.luanti.luanti/.minetest/games/minetest_game`.

These are derivative works of that game's art, not new art. The
upscale is conditioned on the source at low denoise so the output
stays the same texture, and the normal and spec maps are derived
from the upscale. They therefore carry the source's licence and its
