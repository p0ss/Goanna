# Goanna v0.15.1-alpha

Shader packs are now chosen where you start or join a world, and the menus
are tidier, with explanations behind small (i) icons.

## Shader packs, easier to find

- **A Shaders choice on Start Game and Join Game.** Under the materials
  choice, pick None (Goanna's own look) or any pack in your shader pack
  folder. Open folder takes you there; add a pack's ZIP or folder, as
  downloaded from Modrinth or CurseForge, and press Look again. The line
  under it says how the pack loaded on your last join, so a pack that does
  not load says so.
- **The same choice in Settings, Graphics,** now right under the graphics
  profile instead of folded away near the bottom. Both places save the one
  setting. A pack's own options stay folded beneath it.
- The choice that picks material packs on Join Game is now called
  Materials, not Graphics.

Shader pack support itself is unchanged from 0.15.0 and still experimental.

## Tidier menus

- **Explanations behind (i) icons.** Setting descriptions, screen subtitles
  and the graphics profile's description now sit behind a small (i) beside
  what they describe. Hover to read one, click to keep it open, or tap on a
  touchscreen. With a controller or keyboard, the explanation shows while
  the setting it belongs to is selected. Read aloud still reads each one
  when you reach its setting.
- Errors, warnings, progress and anything you need to read before acting
  (download sizes, setup steps, the licence and hosting notes) stay on
  screen.
- **Start Game with an existing world** no longer shifts every row below
  the world name one column out of line.

## Graphics

- **Texture filtering: Crisp or Soft** (Settings, Graphics, Materials).
  Soft blends between texture sizes as Minecraft does, which softens
  distant ground seen at a low angle; blocks up close keep their pixels
  either way. Crisp, the default, is unchanged. The difference is small on
  surfaces with strong relief, where the relief maps decide how sharp the
  distance looks.

## How it was checked

Godot 4.5.1, headless with scratch settings: the new menu shader pack
test (18 checks) and info icon test (28 checks) pass, as do the shader
pack, read aloud, menu, controller and form tests. The menus were
photographed at 100 and 200 per cent on a software renderer, and Soft
filtering was compared with Crisp on an RTX 3090 through the render
service on Mineclonia, Luanti 5.17.0: identical up close, differing
increasingly with distance. Not tried with a real controller, on a Steam
Deck touchscreen, or on Windows; the in-game settings panel was tested but
not photographed.

Goanna is independent of the Luanti project.
