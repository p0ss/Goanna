# Goanna v0.16.0-alpha

Co-op fortresses for DorfCraft: the overseer now shows which house holds
each plan, room and chest, and has a Houses page for running a shared
fortress. The game installer offers a new Kindred Holdfast preview with
co-op houses. `K` flies as in the vanilla client, and the `F` free camera
key is gone. This release is Linux only; there is no Windows build.

## Houses in the overseer

On a DorfCraft server, several players can share one fortress, each as a
house: the player and their workers, with a shared surname and a house
colour drawn in the fortress's background pattern.

- **The Houses page,** a new section on the overseer's rail. It lists each
  house with its colour, its head ("Poss of house Poss") and its workers.
  - The fortress owner can open the fortress to settlers, and banish a
    member after a confirm step.
  - Offices can be filled or vacated from a picker, including by heads of
    houses.
  - Your own workers have labour checkboxes and a workshop picker. Streams
    of work are not available yet.
  - The head, or the owner, can rename a house.
  - Mark shared, Mark locked and Clear mark set access on chests,
    workshops and other things your house built. Shared things anyone may
    use but nobody may dig; locked things only your house may use.
- **House colours on the map.** Plans, rooms and zones that belong to a
  house are filled faintly with the fortress pattern in that house's
  colour. The owner's own plans look as before.
- **A Houses overlay** (footer toggle) tints the map by which house holds
  each cell, with a dark line where two houses meet. The owner's house
  shows paler and striped. While it is on it replaces the pattern fills.
- **An Access overlay** (footer toggle) rings shared cells in the house
  colour and marks locked cells with a solid corner.
- **Inspect and Plans name the house,** for example "Issued by ben (House
  Reed)" or "Locked: only House Reed may use it".
- The overseer's first open after founding no longer drops with
  "connection timeout" while the first view is built.

## Kindred Holdfast preview

Get ready to play and Content offer Kindred Holdfast (preview)
e394015697fc, which brings co-op houses. Use a fresh world: saved worlds
may not carry over between previews. It still needs Mineclonia 0.123.1.

## Controls

- **`K` flies, as in the vanilla client** (D-pad left on a controller).
  It turns Luanti's own fly mode on and off, keeps collision and needs the
  server's `fly` privilege.
- **The `F` free camera key is gone.** It left the body behind and passed
  through terrain, which a vanilla client does not offer. The camera
  remains as a development view behind the control channel.

## Known limits

- No Windows build in this release. Windows clients are not offered the
  update and stay on 0.15.1.
- After quitting, a server may refuse the same player with "already
  connected" for up to about two minutes.
