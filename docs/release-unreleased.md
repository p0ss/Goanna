# Goanna next release (unreleased)

Sunsets light the land again, water reacts to you, and you can now step
out into third person and watch your character walk, swim and tread water.

## Sky and light

- **Golden hour is back.** Sunsets light the land in gold again. Behind
  hills it comes a little earlier, as the sun drops behind them.
- **Clouds at different heights.** Clouds now sit in up to three layers,
  with larger, softer banks in the distance and coverage that changes with
  the weather. Choose one, two or three layers under Advanced, Lighting,
  Cloud layers.
- **A richer night sky.** A galactic band, coloured and twinkling stars,
  a few constellations and planets, and the occasional meteor. The sky
  turns as the night goes on, and twilight gains a violet upper sky.

## Water

- **Water that reacts to you.** Swimming and wading leave a bow wave and a
  wake, falling in sends out rings, and waves settle and bounce off banks.
  Animals make their own smaller waves.
- **Splashes.** Jumping in throws up droplets, climbing out drips, and
  hitting the water splashes where the blow lands.
- **Water that fits its place.** Beaches and reefs are clear, lakes and
  rivers carry silt, and swamps are murky and green. Under water it is
  clear near the surface and murkier with depth, and sunlight through the
  ripples dances on the bed.
- **Waves sized to the water.** Ponds lie nearly still; the open sea rolls.
- **Cleaner reflections** on water, by day and by night.

## Your character and camera

- **Third person.** `F7` switches between first person, behind you and in
  front of you. Hold `Alt` and move the mouse to look around your character
  (from first person this steps out behind you), and use `Alt` with the
  mouse wheel to bring the camera closer.
- **Your character moves.** Your body now walks, runs and sneaks with the
  game's own animations, bends at the knees and elbows, swims the crawl and
  treads water in deep water.
- **Looking down** in first person shows the front of your body, not the
  inside of your neck.

## Materials

- **Glass reflects** in every game, not only stained glass.
- **Gems sparkle.** Emerald, amethyst, mese and quartz get the polished,
  glinting look diamond has.
- **Ice is ice** in more games, including Asuna and Kythen.
- **Plants show their colour.** Sugar cane and other plants are no longer
  drawn blue grey or black at dusk.

## Menu

- **New menu backgrounds.** One of three scenes at random each time you
  start: a coastal village at sunset, a lantern lit street and a lava pool
  in a birch forest.

## Fixes

- Changing the graphics preset during play no longer risks freezing the
  machine; sliders apply when you let go of them.
- Leaves no longer flicker where two blocks meet.
- No more flashing blue sheet where you dig under water.
- In Asuna, still water no longer turns to lava, and lily pads and cave
  vines no longer draw as water.
- Flowers and saplings no longer show a neighbour's texture.
- On Linux, if a GPU hang freezes the desktop, Alt+SysRq+F now ends just
  the game.

## Known issues

- A distant swamp looks like the water you are standing in until you get
  close to it.
- Texture packs that recolour gems lose the gem effect on them.
- In third person the camera jumps in and out when a wall comes between
  it and you.
- More cloud layers cost some performance; choose fewer on a slower
  machine.
